AIM · SHOOT · PLAY

Pool night

Clear your seven solids or stripes, then pocket the 8 ball. You break; the opponent takes turns automatically.

Aim angles update as you move the cue.

Drag anywhere on the felt to aim. Pull the power handle downward and release to shoot. A short pull cancels. After a foul, use Place cue ball to move it onto clear felt.

Game rules

Groups are assigned on the first legal pocket after the break. Hit your group first; after clearing it, hit the 8 ball first. A legal pocket of your group keeps your turn. Missing changes turns.

Scratching, missing every object ball, or hitting the wrong group first gives the other player ball in hand. Pocketing the 8 ball too early, or with a foul, loses. An 8 ball on the break is respotted. This casual version omits called pockets and the rail-contact rule.

The angles behind the shot

The dashed guide shows the cue ball’s first contact. The short line from an object ball shows its initial direction along the line joining the ball centers. An off-center collision splits motion between the balls. Cushions reverse and reduce the perpendicular velocity component. The overlay measures incidence and rebound from the normal; the game’s cushion losses mean these two angles can differ. Aim is measured counterclockwise from the rightward horizontal; cut angle compares incoming direction to the line of centers at ball contact.

Illustrative 2D physics: equal-mass balls, friction and cushion losses; no spin, jump shots, or swerve. The opponent chooses target balls and pockets geometrically.

Keyboard controls

Use the aim and power sliders, or focus the table and press arrows to rotate the cue (Shift for fine aim). Focus the pull control and use arrows to change power; Enter shoots. Take shot also works.
