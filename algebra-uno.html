A LITTLE ALGEBRA AT THE CARD TABLE

Algebra UNO

You vs. the opponent · combine like terms to play your cards.

Cards designed by Lenin Ortiz · Instagram: @leninortiz28

How to play
  1. Each player starts with five cards. Be the first to empty your hand.
  2. Match the active color, a like term, or the same signed coefficient (for example, 3x can follow 3y): the variable and exponent must both match. Green = x, Blue = y, Red = x², Yellow = y². Like terms may have different coefficients. A coefficient match with different variables or exponents is an unlike-term play: choose “Keep both terms.”
  3. When you play a term on another like term, add their coefficients. Enter only the resulting coefficient: 3x + (−2x) = x, so enter 1. The reduction checks your play; the new top card keeps its printed value. After an action card, enter your term’s own coefficient.
  4. A nonmatching card costs one drawn card and ends your turn. For a calculation, you get one retry; a second incorrect answer costs one drawn card and ends your turn. You keep the card you tried to play. If you draw a matching card, you can play it immediately, or keep it and end your turn. The opponent follows the same draw-and-play rule.
  5. Skip and Reverse skip the other player in this two-player version. The printed +4 card makes the other player draw two and lose a turn, following the supplied activity’s rule. Both +4 and Wild let you choose the next color. Action cards do not need a calculation.
  6. UNO is called automatically for both players when one card remains. There is no forgotten-UNO penalty.
  7. The opponent follows the same matching rules, solves its reductions automatically, and draws when it cannot play, then plays the drawn card immediately if it matches. Used cards are reshuffled when the draw pile runs out.

Adapted from the supplied “Reducción de términos semejantes” activity by Lenin Ortiz. Digital two-player adaptation: the printed +4 follows the source’s draw-two rule. Original card artwork and colors are preserved.
