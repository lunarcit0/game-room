The model & the math

Horizontal component vx = v cos(θ); upward component vy = v sin(θ). The screen’s y coordinate increases downward. At a vertical cushion vx changes sign; at a horizontal cushion vy changes sign. The incidence and reflection angles are measured from a line perpendicular to the cushion (the normal).

One ball only; no spin or ball-to-ball collisions. Cushions reflect perfectly. Motion slows under a fixed, illustrative deceleration; shot strength controls starting speed. Pockets capture within a fixed radius. These are teaching assumptions, not predictions for a physical pool table.
